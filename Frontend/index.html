Get Started How it works ↓

A few simple steps

Lost. Found. Reconnected.

One place on campus to report missing belongings, discover found items, and manage return requests.

Step 1

Report

Post an item with its name, category, description, location and date.

📍 The more detail, the easier it is to recognise.

Step 2

Search

Browse the board and search by item name or location.

🔎 Found items matching your lost post show up first.

Step 3

Reconnect

Send a claim request to contact the finder.

🤝 The finder's contact details appear after your request is approved.
